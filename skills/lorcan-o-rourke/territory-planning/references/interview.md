# Interview: scoring first

Inspect supplied context, existing models and accessible fields before asking. Follow the workspace's
account-scoring conventions: known population, Workflows, reuse evidence and no Tables. Do not ask for
business questions already answered in Knowledge Hub, but flag material conflicts instead of choosing
silently. Supplied customer rules take precedence over suggested defaults.

First establish the decision: what the team sells, who buys, what drives account value, a good account,
a poor-fit account, and whether the request is rubric-only or a build. Discover the population's fields
and source IDs. Ask only for missing business judgments and authorization. A new customer does not
need to know the term 'rubric'. Offer a short starting proposal.

A known account is a domain + stable ID + expected grade + why. Five should include a strong, weak,
boundary and uncertain case when possible. A lost deal is negative fit evidence only when its loss
reason relates to fit. Do not label missing outcome data as a lost deal. Never assign creator-run
example scores to today's companies as an expected truth.

Turn business judgment into a few distinct factors. Each needs a data need and a reason it can change
the decision. Do not force eight factors on every business. Tech, funding, growth, sales headcount and
hierarchy are optional. Correlated facts need a group cap or reduced weight, not duplicate credit.
Weights and grades are proposed heuristics until approved. Separate commercial exclusions from fit.

Before research establish permitted sources and freshness. Ask only for the missing budget or approval
needed by the host policy. Do not expose a balance merely because a command returned one.

Before carving, and not before scoring: ask about book capacity, routing buckets, existing owners,
customer/opportunity protections, exclusions, shared reps, and what fairness means. Count balance is
not revenue balance. Account families need an agreed grain and verified ownership evidence; otherwise
the candidate supports entity-level preview only. If protection fields are unavailable, stop carving
rather than silently disabling protection.

Before optional CRM work inspect the chosen connection and field schema. Confirm the organization
and environment from actual metadata or the administrator. Alias wording is not a safety check.
A reviewer or creator's teammate can confirm business policy for this run; do not attribute newly
inferred intent to the original author without the author's confirmation.
